lore:
in the begining, there only existed the thars, the gods who created everything. nash'thar made creatures out of itself, taking pieces of itself and molding living beings out of it. mak'thar breathed life onto them. rath'thar, along with sav'thar, made death to bring meaning to the creatures.
the creatures made by the thars where sent to thar'eke to fufil a life and bring entretainment and boons to the thars. thareke is a barren land, things from earth such as grass and other such forms of nature are called makeke, and are extremely scarce. the lands are mostly covered in raw soil and the skies, called ato, are black like tar, with light comming from mostly fires made by humans, called make'nash.
at the begning, the makenash lived in small clans, fully tribal all looking for the thars aproval. notable from this era was Rath'Thie, a make'nash who was most famous for being a the first make'thar, a vessel of rath'thar, the first make'nash to use thar'ezo.
Rath'Thiel was born in a small clan who worshiped rath'thar. he was first born to the clan's ezo'nash and was to be sacrifieced at birth, but, as he was laid as a baby to be sacrificed, rath'thar took a form of pitty in Rath'Thiel, as he was set on fire, Rath'Thiel started to burn, but, he wouldn't perish. when the fire stoped, Rath'thie was covered in burns in all but the left side of his face, where there was instead a sigil of rath'thar, a tal'rath, indicating that Rath'Thiel was now rath'thar's.
Rath'Thiel, went on travels orderd by rath'thar with the goals of both spreading rath'thar's influence and rath'ezo, the first kind of ezo to be spread across thar'eke.
The spread of rath'ezo across thar'eke lead to the teaching of more ezos by the thars and the birth of more make'thars, notables being: Nash'Thiel, son of Rath'Thiel and make'thar of nash'thar. Mak'Aste, the second ever make'nash, of the thar mak'thar. And last but not least, Sav'Mika, the first make'thar of sav'thar
